Policy Desk

where the federal government is actually putting money — scored against SPY, not against the hype
Washington now takes equity, penny warrants, price floors, offtake and revenue shares in listed companies. Rather than list the announcements, this desk runs a market model event study on them: alpha and beta fitted on the 250 sessions before each deal, abnormal return cumulated over the windows after it. Day 0 is the announcement session — gapped, and mostly unbuyable. Every other window starts at the day-0 close, the first price you could actually pay. The deals are grouped by structure, because the question worth answering is not whether policy pays but which kind of deal pays.
